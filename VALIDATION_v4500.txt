7. Cache migration note
PARSER_VERSION was intentionally bumped from 4070 to 4500.
Reason: old Launcher IndexedDB cache contains Juggler-only parsed days. Keeping 4070 would cause those dates to be skipped and HANA rows never collected.
Tool-side old imported data remains compatible; new combined days may replace the same shop/date when imported.

Known modeling limitation
Lamp/small-role probabilities include estimated/empirical values as previously agreed. They are treated as the working probabilities for v4.5.0. This validation confirms implementation consistency, not manufacturer confirmation of every probability.

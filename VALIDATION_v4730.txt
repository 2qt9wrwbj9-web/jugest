JUGGLER / HANA HANA TOOL v4.7.3 VALIDATION — RELAY RECEIVE INIT FIX

Scope
-----
Bug-fix-only follow-up to v4.7.1. No prediction/judgement mathematics or acquisition behavior is intentionally changed.

Fixed
-----
- Tool-side Launcher relay receiving could hit Safari/JavaScript TDZ error:
  Cannot access 'externalStorageReadyPromise' before initialization
- Root cause: relayReceiveAll() awaited a lexical const whose initializer lived near the end of the startup script.
- Fix: create a deferred externalStorageReadyPromise readiness gate immediately after initExternalStorage() is defined, before relay receive handlers can await it. The actual initExternalStorage() call remains at the original late-startup phase and resolves the gate in finally(), preserving restore/UI initialization order.
